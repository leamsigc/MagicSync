<section class="mx-auto prose dark:prose-invert pt-6 text-sm md:text-xl">

## Sales and product questions

Email **[MagicSync@leamsigc.com](mailto:MagicSync@leamsigc.com)** with the subject **Sales**. Tell us how many brands you manage, which networks matter most and whether you need cloud or self-hosted. We reply with a straight recommendation, including when a competitor is a better fit.

## Support and troubleshooting

Email **[MagicSync@leamsigc.com](mailto:MagicSync@leamsigc.com)** with the subject **Support** and include:

- the email address on your account;
- the post ID or affected account;
- the platform and approximate time with your time zone;
- the exact error text or a screenshot.

Before writing, check the [status page](/status) and the [support answers](/support). Most failed publishes are an expired token or a rejected media format, both of which you can fix in the integrations screen.

## Partnerships and agencies

Use the subject **Partners** and include your active client count, the networks you publish to and how approvals work today. See the [partner program](/partners) for what is included.

## Press and open source

For interviews, screenshots or product context, use the subject **Press**. Source code, issues and contribution details live on [GitHub](https://github.com/leamsigc/MagicSync).

## What we do not do by email

We do not publish posts on your behalf, accept social account passwords, or bypass platform review processes. Account security stays with you.

</section>
