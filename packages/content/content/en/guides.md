## Recommended starting points

- **New to scheduling?** Read [How to Schedule Social Media Posts Free](/blogs/how-to-schedule-social-media-posts-free) and publish your first week today.
- **Already posting, but inconsistent?** Use the [content repurposing guide](/blogs/ai-content-repurposing-multi-platform) to turn one idea into a full week.
- **Running a launch?** Follow the [bulk import walkthrough](/blogs/bulk-import-social-media-creation) to prepare a CSV and schedule the campaign in batches.
- **Exploring AI agents?** Read [Social Media AI Agents and Automation](/blogs/social-media-ai-agents-automation) before enabling approval-gated publishing.
- **Developer or operator?** Start with [API keys and programmable social media](/blogs/magicsync-api-keys-programmable-social-media).
- **Making video content?** Learn [how to remove silence from video](/blogs/how-to-remove-silence-from-video) with the built-in tool.

## A simple weekly rhythm

1. **Monday:** review analytics and pick the two posts that earned the most replies.
2. **Tuesday:** draft the week's posts in the composer, reusing the winning formats.
3. **Wednesday:** approve drafts and fill any calendar gaps.
4. **Thursday:** answer comments and DMs from the unified inbox.
5. **Friday:** schedule one repurposed post for the weekend and note what to test next week.

## What makes a guide worth following

Ask three questions before adopting any social media advice: does it name the platform, does it show the actual step, and does it explain how to tell whether it worked? If a guide cannot answer all three, it is entertainment, not a process.
