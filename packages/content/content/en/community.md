- **LinkedIn** — longer marketing and engineering posts at [linkedin.com/in/magicsyncdotdev](https://www.linkedin.com/in/magicsyncdotdev/).
- **GitHub** — source, issues and feature requests at [github.com/leamsigc/MagicSync](https://github.com/leamsigc/MagicSync).

A dedicated Discord is in preparation. Until it opens, the blog comments and GitHub issues are the fastest way to reach the team in public.

## What we ask of members

1. **Show the work.** A screenshot of the calendar or the actual caption teaches more than a generic opinion.
2. **Name the platform.** Advice that works on LinkedIn often fails on Pinterest.
3. **Report the failure honestly.** Publishing bugs are easier to fix when the post ID and platform are included.
4. **No spam.** Links to your own offers are fine when they answer someone's question. Drive-by promotion is not.

## Community-built improvements

Many of the product's sharp edges were found by users: the need for a per-platform first comment, clearer failed-post errors, better calendar density on mobile and a way to group accounts by client. Each one started as a community report, not an internal roadmap meeting.
