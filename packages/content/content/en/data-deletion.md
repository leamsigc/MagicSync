# Data Deletion Instructions

This page explains how to delete data MagicSync holds about you, including data received from Meta platforms (Facebook, Instagram) when you connect your accounts.

## What Data MagicSync Stores

- Your account profile (name, email)
- OAuth access tokens for connected social media accounts
- Posts, schedules, media, and campaign settings you create
- Engagement data (comments, messages) shown in your inbox
- Notification and audit log entries

## Delete Data Yourself (Immediate)

1. **Disconnect a social account** — Go to **Connect accounts** (`/app/integrations`) and disconnect the account. This revokes and deletes the stored OAuth token immediately. MagicSync can no longer read, publish, or message on that account.
2. **Delete content** — Remove posts, media files, templates, and auto-reply campaigns from the app. Deleted items are removed from our database.
3. **Clear inbox items** — Delete or archive inbox entries you no longer need.

## Request Full Account Erasure

To request complete deletion of your MagicSync account and all associated data (including Meta Platform Data), email **privacy@magicsync.dev** from the email address linked to your account with the subject **"Delete my data"**.

- We confirm receipt within 7 days and complete erasure within 30 days.
- Erasure covers your profile, connected-account tokens, posts, media, campaigns, inbox items, notifications, and logs.
- Aggregated, fully anonymized analytics that cannot identify you may be retained.

## Data Received From Meta

When you connect Facebook or Instagram, Meta shares your public profile, Pages, media, comments, and conversations needed for scheduling, inbox, and auto-reply features. This Platform Data is:

- Used only to provide the features you explicitly enable
- Never sold or shared with third parties for marketing
- Deleted automatically when you disconnect the account or when your erasure request is completed

## Contact Us

Questions about data deletion: **privacy@magicsync.dev**.
