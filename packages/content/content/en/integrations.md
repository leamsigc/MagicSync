## Supported networks

**Social feeds:** Facebook, Instagram, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Pinterest, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, Bluesky, Reddit, Mastodon, Dribbble and Snapchat.

**Local and community:** Google Business Profile, Discord and Facebook Groups.

**Publishing platforms:** WordPress and Dev.to.

Platform-specific setup guides live in the [platform section](/platform). For a deeper technical view, see the [API reference](/api-reference).

## What happens when a network changes its rules?

Social APIs change constantly. MagicSync tracks those changes centrally so you do not have to rewrite your workflow every quarter. When a capability is temporarily unavailable, the affected post is flagged before publishing instead of failing after the fact.
