## Annual billing

Pro is **$29 per month**, or **$290 per year** when you pay annually — roughly two months free. You can switch billing frequency from the billing page at any time.

## Enterprise and agencies

Managing dozens of client brands? Enterprise adds custom limits, onboarding, white-label reporting and a named contact. [Talk to us](/contact) about volume pricing and data residency requirements.

## What is included in every plan

| Included | Self-Hosted | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Connected networks | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Scheduled posts | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| AI content generation | Yes | Yes |
| Visual calendar | Yes | Yes |
| Unified inbox | Yes | Yes |
| Analytics dashboard | Yes | Yes |
| Bulk CSV import | Yes | Yes |
| Managed hosting | No | Yes |
| Automatic upgrades | You run them | Included |
| Support | Community | Priority email |

## Fair-use limits

We do not throttle normal business usage or charge per published post. Abuse, resale of API access and automated scraping are outside the fair-use policy in the [terms of service](/terms-of-use).
